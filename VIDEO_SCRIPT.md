# NOVA Video Script

**Target Length:** 3-5 minutes

## 0:00–0:20 - Problem
*Visual: A busy desk, scattered printed notices, open laptop, and a smartphone.*
**Voiceover:** "As students and professionals, our context is scattered. We see a notice on a wall, we have a deadline on our laptop, and we need our phone to bridge the gap. But traditional voice assistants are blind to our context. They wait for rigid commands."

## 0:20–0:45 - Solution (NOVA)
*Visual: Open the NOVA app on Android. Dark, premium UI.*
**Voiceover:** "Meet NOVA. An offline contextual action layer for your smartphone. NOVA doesn't just listen; it sees, understands, and acts locally."

## 0:45–1:30 - Camera + Voice Demo ("Handle This")
*Visual: User taps 'See', points at a 'Project Review' notice. Taps 'Speak' and says "Handle this."*
**Voiceover:** "Instead of manually creating a reminder, just point your camera and say 'Handle this.' NOVA's on-device OCR extracts the text, and its local intent engine understands the event and deadline. It proposes the safest action—creating a reminder—and asks for confirmation."

## 1:30–2:15 - Context Understanding + Reminder
*Visual: Screen shows extracted event details: 'Project Review, Oct 7, 11 AM, Bring PPT'. User confirms.*
**Voiceover:** "Everything you see here is processed offline. No cloud delays. No privacy leaks. The reminder is set natively."

## 2:15–3:00 - Project/File Workflow
*Visual: Back to home screen. User says: "Find my latest presentation."*
**Voiceover:** "NOVA also understands your local file system. It finds 'ProjectReview_v7.pptx' instantly."

## 3:00–3:35 - Phone → Laptop / Office Kit
*Visual: User says: "Send it to my laptop." Screen shows 'Sent to laptop' via OfficeKit Bridge.*
**Voiceover:** "Because NOVA is designed to integrate with the Office Kit abstraction, transferring context from phone to PC is seamless. Our current prototype isolates this behind an adapter interface, proving the flow so that a single command pushes the file directly to your workstation in the final build."

## 3:35–4:10 - Offline / Local AI Explanation
*Visual: Show the Settings menu, toggling 'Airplane Mode', proving the app stays 'Local AI Ready'.*
**Voiceover:** "The magic is in the architecture. SEE, SAY, THINK, DO. Our current prototype establishes the offline-first architecture and demonstrates the real camera, OCR, and action loop. The remaining adapters are isolated behind interfaces for the official build phase."

## 4:10–4:30 - Closing
*Visual: NOVA logo fades in over black.*
**Voiceover:** "NOVA. Turn everyday context into intelligent action."
